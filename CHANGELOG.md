# Changelog

What changed in each version of this extension, newest first.

## 0.1.0

- Define your own Prometheus dashboards of PromQL panels, listed under clusters in the navigator.
- Show a dashboard on one cluster, mirror it to chosen clusters or to all current and future clusters, or copy it to other clusters.
- Open a dashboard in a tab or in a window of its own.
- Draw a panel as a time series, stacked area, bar gauge, stat, gauge, donut or table.
- Give a panel its own time range, width and height.
- Start from 46 ready-made panels across the cluster, nodes, namespaces, workloads, control plane, storage and network.
- Edit panels with a live preview.
- Read every series' value at any moment by hovering over a time series.
- Click a pod, node, workload, volume claim or namespace in a chart or its legend to open its details.
